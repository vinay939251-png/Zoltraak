# Policy management

Policy documents are versioned records with checksum, source, access classification, effective date, approval metadata, and traceable chunks. Only approved active demonstration versions may be retrieved for copilot evidence. A draft cannot influence eligibility. Superseded policy versions remain available for historical audit but are excluded from current answers unless a historical question explicitly requests them.

The local fixture includes active and superseded demonstration policies. It does not permit ordinary collector policy edits; a future administrator workflow must require draft creation, review, approval, activation, and audit records as separate transitions.
