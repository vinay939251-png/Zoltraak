# Experiment plan

The intended comparison is a copilot-assisted treatment cohort against a manual control cohort. Assignment must be recorded before outcome observation, stratified by product and DPD, and protected against the same account entering both groups.

**Thirty-day cure rate** is cured eligible accounts divided by the frozen eligible starting population. **PTP adherence** is mature kept promises divided by mature eligible promises, with rescheduled, canceled, disputed, and not-yet-due promises handled explicitly. **Contacts per cure** is qualifying contacts divided by cured accounts, with both numerator and denominator shown.

The local fixture exposes the metric shape and status as `DEMO_FIXTURE_NOT_MATURE`; it does not claim causal impact, statistical significance, confidence intervals, or business improvement.
